How it works

  • The bars show how busy a spot is, from reports in the last hour: green is empty, yellow is some seats, red is packed.
  • Tap a spot to report how busy it is, see its hours, find food nearby, or start a study timer.
  • Can't decide? Tap Pick a spot for me.
  • Reports, favorites, and notes are saved only on this device.

Your location stays on this device and isn't saved. Distances are straight lines, not walking routes.
